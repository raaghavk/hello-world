## 🗣️ Communication Strategy

### How to Talk to Indian SMBs

**Use Simple Language** (They're "dumb" as you said - meaning not tech-savvy):

❌ **DON'T SAY:**
- "We monitor your server uptime with HTTP GET requests"
- "99.99% SLA with automated incident management"
- "RESTful API with webhook integrations"

✅ **DO SAY:**
- "We check if your website is working every 5 minutes"
- "We message you on WhatsApp if your site is down"
- "You'll never lose a sale because your website stopped working"

### Landing Page Message (in simple English/Hindi)

**Headline:**
"Is Your Website Working Right Now?"
"अभी आपकी वेबसाइट काम कर रही है?"

**Subheadline:**
"Get instant WhatsApp alert if your website goes down. Never lose a customer again."
"वेबसाइट बंद होने पर तुरंत व्हाट्सएप पर संदेश पाएं। कोई ग्राहक न खोएं।"

**Benefits (in simple language):**
- 💰 "Don't lose money when your site is down"
- 📱 "Get WhatsApp message in 1 minute"
- 😊 "Keep your customers happy"
- 🔧 "Fix problems before customers complain"
